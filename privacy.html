GPS HSE Department — Privacy

These pages (Staff Requirement Portal, vacancy request form and the candidate document upload) are internal tools of the HSE Department of Gulf Petrochemical Services & Trading LLC (GPS), Oman.

What we use from Google sign-in

Only your name and e-mail address, to confirm who you are and which project you may see. We do not read your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data. You can also sign in with a code sent to your e-mail instead.

What we keep

What you submit — vacancy requests and decisions, or, for candidates, the documents you upload — is stored in GPS Google Sheets and Google Drive for HSE recruitment only. It is not sold or shared outside GPS. Candidate upload links work only for the candidate they were sent to and expire after 60 days.
